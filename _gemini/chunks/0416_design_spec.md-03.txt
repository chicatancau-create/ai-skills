- **Icon Treatment**: Use one coherent icon family at consistent weight, only to mark recurring capability classes, platforms, or states. Keep an icon's meaning fixed across the deck. Avoid icon grids used as a feature list, third-party logos as decoration, and mixed icon languages on one page.
